## What was built and how (method)
Everything was modelled freestyle in Blender through `tools/ae_kit.py` (section "pass 4", entry point `build_all4()`), exported to
`kit/own/ae_kit_r4.fbx` and imported with Import 3D (Insert Using Scene Position + Anchored). The kit is authored
**in plaza coordinates** (floor y=0, terrace y=6, -Z = north), so `fix/build_plaza.lua` places the big pieces at the
identity CFrame with no layout maths.

| piece | method | meshes (by material) |
|---|---|---|
| Hero (4 corners) | authored ONE corner (NE) in a diagonal frame `P(u, y, w)` (w = out along the diagonal, u = lateral), then `merge_objs(..., mirror4=True)` bakes the X and Z mirrors into one mesh per material (normals flipped on the mirrored copies) | `K_Hero_stone, darkStone, gold, goldLan, blue, glass, water` |
| Stairs (4 flights) | one north flight authored, `rot4=True` adds the 3 rotations | `K_Stairs_stone, blue, gold, glass` |
| Floor + medallion | dais, bands, inlays authored once; the per-arm runner inlay and foot band authored for N and rotated x4 | `K_Medal_pale, slate, gold, blue, darkStone` |
| Bollard | small separate piece, placed 8 times | `K_Bollard_white, gold` |
| Plants | existing puff tree / bush / grass pieces, placed with tint per zone | |

## Lessons (for the workflow vault)
1. **Author the kit in world coordinates when the piece is symmetric.** Building one quarter in the plaza frame and baking the mirrors means placement is the identity CFrame. Layout bugs vanish, and every number in the report is a plaza coordinate you can check against the top view.
2. **Use a diagonal helper frame for corner pieces.** `P(u, y, w)` (out along the diagonal, lateral, height) turns "facing the centre on the diagonal" into plain numbers. Mirroring needs `flip_normals()` on the copies with a negative determinant.
3. **Import 3D clamps every MeshPart to 2048 studs by shrinking the WHOLE import.** The 124-stud hero x 40.5585 = 5029, so everything came in at 16.516 instead of 40.5585. Never hard-code the import scale: measure it from a piece of known size (`build_plaza.lua` uses the 40-stud dais).
4. **Check normals on extruded concave outlines.** `extrude_outline` produced the hooked blades inside-out: in Roblox the top faces were culled and the bottoms sat under the medallion, so only the side walls showed as thin arcs. Check top-face normals in Blender (count faces at the top height with normal z > 0) before exporting.
5. **Triangulate before export.** It is cheap and removes any doubt about how Studio triangulates concave n-gons (notched blocks, hooked blades).
6. **Keep each MeshPart under 20k triangles.** Round tube trims add up fast: the hero gold came to 24.9k, so the lantern gold is split into `goldLan` (the colour lookup matches by prefix, so it still shades gold).
7. **Measure rendered pixels, not part colours.** With the reference's Brightness 3.5 and flat SmoothPlastic, a 176-grey floor rendered at 232-255 (clipped). Sample the same pixel in the reference and in the build and set colours about 25-35% under the picture values.
8. **A Roblox `Texture` covers the part colour.** Tint it with `Texture.Color3`, otherwise every textured floor renders white whatever the part colour.
9. **Watch large planes near a sunken area.** An 800-stud water plane at y 2.5 flooded the sunken plaza; keep such planes below the lowest walkable floor.
10. **Read stair geometry from the horizon.** Where the stair top meets the eye-height horizon in a spawn view gives the terrace height; the top view gives the depth. Together they give the step count and rise without any coordinates.
11. **Import 3D is GUI-only and fragile.** Malwarebytes steals focus (wait 4 s, AppActivate, retry). Key presses were interrupted several times, but clicks went through, so the reliable recipe is: click the file box, type the path, click **Open** (not Enter), tick the two boxes, zoom to confirm, click Import. Then confirm arrival from Luau (count the MeshParts under `workspace.ae_kit_r4`) instead of watching the screen.
12. **Fix brightness before geometry details.** In this pass the fastest visual gains after the hero piece came from colour and exposure fixes, not geometry; reviewers read value first.
